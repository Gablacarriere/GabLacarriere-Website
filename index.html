EXHIBIT 01 / THE DUETTwo dancers. One shared space.

Notice how they change direction together while keeping their own space.

Watch a phrase, then compare another way of responding.

A question worth dancing

Two voices.
A dance between them.

Press play and watch the spheres dance. Try an echo, then give them room to play. What changes when they stop doing exactly the same thing?

The point isn’t to find one “correct” duet. It’s to notice timing, shared direction, and space for a response. In a lesson, we explore those choices through real movement and listening.

IN YOUR LESSON

“Why does this step feel rushed?”

You bring a real question. Together, we notice when your weight arrives and what happens just before the next step.

You leave with: one clear thing to pay attention to.

The lesson is a beginning

Your dancing has
more to discover.

Follow one example: a step that feels rushed. See how a question in a lesson can become an experiment, a practice, and eventually a connection to the rest of your dancing.

Zoukable helps you practice between lessons. Your coach helps you understand what changes. Atlas connects the discoveries you have explored together. You can return to any stage—there’s no race to finish.

New here or need help?

Your first visit

Open your invitation email and follow its link to choose your password. Then sign in with the same email address that received the invitation.

I can’t find my invitation.

Search your inbox for “You’ve been invited” and check Spam or Promotions. If it is missing, ask Gab for a fresh invitation using the email address you want to sign in with.

My link has expired, or I forgot my password.

On the sign-in form, enter your invited email address and select “Forgot your password?” Open the newest reset email to choose a new password. If that does not work, email Gab for help.

Where should I start after signing in?

Open your Zouk Atlas and choose My practice for your latest coach-reviewed lesson focus. The hub’s Practice section holds your training tools and weekly reflection. Return to Today whenever you need your starting point.

Why is my map empty or only partly discovered?

Your map shows concepts Gab or Steph has recorded from your lessons. A hidden star means it has not been recorded yet; it does not mean you cannot do it. You can explore the stars already discovered while your next lesson is being reviewed.

Do practice points unlock stars?

Practice activity and XP are separate from your concept map. Your coaches record discoveries from your lessons. Exploring the map does not change your recorded level.

Still stuck? Email Gab with the page name and what happened. Never include your password or your invitation link.

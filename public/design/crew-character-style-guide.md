# Crew character design — Version 3
September 11, 2026. Supersedes the earlier character-style instructions where they differ.

## Shared art direction
Original human game characters inspired by Monument Valley's simple sculptural shapes, quiet compositions and restrained colors, and Animal Crossing's rounded proportions, friendliness and individual personality. Keep a small trace of Ranking of Kings and The Little Prince in the explorer costumes and poetic adventure theme.
Do not copy named game characters, their signature costumes or game assets. The crew represent real adults, not children or animals.

## Shape and rendering
Full body, roughly 2.5 heads tall; oversized rounded head, compact torso, short rounded limbs and visible boots. One prop per role. Facial identity comes from head shape, hairstyle, brows and a few simplified features.
Soft matte volumes, broad shadow shapes, subtle edges rather than thick comic outlines. No photorealistic pores, elaborate clothing textures or tiny ornaments.
The current generated pair lean toward Animal Crossing's rounded warmth. For future small sprites, continue reducing detail toward Monument Valley's clarity.
Do not turn staff into faceless generic explorers. Students may be simpler, but use the same palette and shape language. Preserve saved student customization.

## Identity invariants
### Gab — Captain
Bald/shaved head beneath a short sand-colored docker hat above the ears. Small tilted gold crown, strong brows, short dark beard and moustache. Sage/olive explorer outfit, ochre scarf, brown boots.
Tattoos ONLY on anatomical right arm and neck. In a frontal view, the tattooed arm is on the viewer's left. Never mirror artwork. Untattooed anatomical left hand holds a brass telescope. Both feet and telescope remain in frame.
### Steph — Co-Captain
Long dark wavy hair, warm olive complexion guided by her supplied photographs, strong brows, subtle winged eyeliner and red lips. Plum outfit, teal scarf, brown boots, small gold star hair clip. Holds a navigation compass. No copied Gab tattoos, crown or docker hat.
### Lorell — Mission Control
Use this drawing system with Lorell's own references; do not invent her likeness from another crew member. Her identity and role remain Mission Control / COO. An operations tablet or communication device is a suitable single prop when her avatar is commissioned.

## Colors
Shared slate ink #293f51, sand #d7b384, sage #788263, plum #6c3d55, teal #39716e, ochre #dcb24f, brown #69482e.
These are palette targets, not sampled measurements. Preserve each person's own skin tone. Do not standardize complexion.

## Website scale and exports
56–72px tall, full body with object-fit:contain. Never display the large image-generation preview as the actual website size.
Current v3 assets have opaque dark navy backgrounds; do not describe them as transparent. Place in the existing small crew slots. Keep full-resolution masters and optimized 216px-tall PNG exports.
Shared source: mentorship-crew.js. Every crew-card and adventure-scene instance must point to the same versioned files. Roles and permissions are separate from illustration.
Still poses by default. Future motion must be gentle, optional and respect reduced motion. No decorative character should cover controls.

Costume: [approved colors]. One prop: [prop]. Preserve identity and anatomical details. Keep the complete silhouette readable at 56–72px. ## Acceptance checks
Recognizable person; correct tattoo side and prop; full silhouette; coherent alongside the other crew at actual size; no assumed role permissions; no fabricated student progress.

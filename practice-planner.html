Interactive training tool

Build your Zouk practice session.

Choose one training problem and generate a focused session around it. The goal is not to practice everything — it is to create a clear learning signal.

Why this tool is structured this way

Isolate the constraint

Focused practice is usually clearer than repeating a full combination while trying to fix five things at once.

→

Know what organizes the movement

Timing, connection, pathway and body organization are different problems and should not always be trained the same way.

→

Use enough embodiment

If you already understand the idea, more physical repetition may be more useful than more explanation.

→
Practice principle

A useful practice session has a specific target, enough repetition to learn from it, and enough variation to test whether the skill transfers.

Need a human diagnosis?

A planner can organize practice. It cannot diagnose everything.

If you are unsure what the real bottleneck is, private training or mentorship can help identify what to work on before you spend time repeating the wrong thing.

How to use them

Prediction before execution.

For most puzzles, ask the learner to state what they recognize, what they predict, what candidates they see, what they choose and where or when they expect to arrive. Then execute once and compare the prediction with reality.

Assessment

Do not score only whether the movement worked.

Use five dimensions when useful: Recognition, Projection / Visualization, Timing / Calculation, Decision Quality and Adaptation. A failed execution can still reveal strong reasoning; an accidental success can hide weak perception.

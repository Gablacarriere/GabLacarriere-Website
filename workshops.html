For organizers

Teaching designed around what dancers should retain.

Clear pedagogy

Concepts are organized so dancers understand what creates the movement, not only what shape to reproduce.

High transfer

Exercises are designed to survive beyond the workshop and transfer into social dancing with different partners.

Adaptable level

Material can be shaped for developing dancers, experienced social dancers, competitors or mixed-level festival groups.

Workshop directions

Topics can be built around the needs of the event.

Movement Architecture

How movements are built, connected and transformed.

Connection & Communication

How information travels through the partnership.

Timing & Rhythm

Using timing as a movement tool in Zouk and Lambada.

Social-Dance Adaptability

Decision-making, variation and partner adaptation.

Technique Through Constraints

Focused constraints and deliberate practice.

Lambada Pathways

Rhythm, organization, pathways and partner communication.

Formats

Single workshop or progressive curriculum.

Workshops can be designed as one class, a multi-class festival track, an intensive or a complete weekender curriculum.

What to send when booking

City, dates, event format, expected dancer level, approximate class length and whether you want Brazilian Zouk, Lambada or both.

Teaching principle

A good workshop should leave dancers with a tool they can still use after they forget the combination.

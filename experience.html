Education & teacher development

A foundation in pedagogy.

Gab holds a master’s degree in pedagogy. His background as a school teacher informs how he observes learners, structures lessons and supports independent learning.

Years of teaching teachers.

Teaching people how to teach has become a specialty of Gab’s work, alongside curriculum design and pedagogical coaching.

This work connects the decisions inside a single lesson with the larger progression of a course.

Career timeline
c. 2017–2018 · Rennes

First teaching years

Early Brazilian Zouk teaching in Rennes, France, alongside Marie Robert, before Gab's teaching work expanded toward Paris.

From 2018 · Paris + Europe

Gab & Sabrina

Several years of regular Brazilian Zouk teaching with Sabrina Beija-Flor, including Paris classes, workshops, social events and European appearances. This period also included Gab's growing work as a Zouk DJ.

Europe

Gab & Patrycja

A long-term Brazilian Zouk and Lambada teaching and performance partnership with Patrycja Jakimczyk, with work across European dance communities.

2023–2024 · International

Gab & Caroline

Collaboration with Caroline Haugsted, later becoming an official dance partnership in 2024. Their work included teaching and creative projects across Europe, Mexico and South America, alongside development of Zouk Art Empowerment.

New York City

Pedagogy-led training

Current work brings together weekly classes, private training, kinesthetic practice, dance mentorship and workshops, alongside teacher development and curriculum design.

What the experience includes

Regular teaching

Long-term class programs designed around progression rather than one-off combinations.

Partner collaborations

Multiple long-term teaching and performance partnerships across different countries and dance communities.

Workshops & festivals

Teaching in event formats where material has to be clear, transferable and useful with limited contact time.

Brazilian Zouk + Lambada

Experience across both dances, with attention to their technical and pedagogical differences.

DJ work

Years of involvement in Zouk culture through music curation as well as teaching and performance.

Movement education

A teaching approach grounded in a master’s degree in pedagogy and continuing work on how people learn.

Current work

New York base. International scope.

Today, Gab works across classes, private training, mentorship, workshops and creative projects. The common thread is movement understanding: identifying what organizes a movement, practicing the right constraint and transferring the skill into spontaneous dancing.

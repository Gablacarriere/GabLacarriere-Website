Inside the work

From a topic to a lesson that makes sense.

An example: you want to teach timing. Together, we can turn that broad topic into a sequence of teaching decisions.

  1. 01

    Define the learning.

    What should students be able to hear, feel or do by the end? Make the goal specific enough to observe.

  2. 02

    Find the starting point.

    Can students hear the pulse? Shift their weight? Coordinate with a partner? Identify what the next step depends on.

  3. 03

    Design the experience.

    Choose an exercise with one clear focus. Decide what to demonstrate, what to ask and what feedback will help.

  4. 04

    Check, adapt and revisit.

    Change the music or partner. Notice what carries over, adjust the lesson and return to the skill in a later class.

This is an example of the work we can explore, rather than a fixed curriculum. Your students and teaching context guide the design.

Before we begin

A few useful answers.

Is this for me if I haven’t started teaching yet?

Yes. Preparing your first class is a useful place to begin. We can work on a clear learning goal, a manageable progression and how to explain or demonstrate the ideas you want to teach.

How is this different from dance mentorship?

Teacher development focuses on how you help other people learn: planning, explanation, observation, feedback and curriculum design. Dance mentorship focuses on your own dancing and practice. If you want to work on both, describe that in your inquiry so we can discuss the right focus.

What should I bring to a first conversation?

A question is enough to start. If you have one, bring a lesson outline, course plan or example of a difficulty you encounter in class. Tell me who your learners are and what you want them to be able to do.

Can a school or teaching team work with you?

Yes. We can discuss a shared teaching challenge, teacher-development work or the design and review of a curriculum. Include the size of your team, your learners’ levels and the program you have in mind.

How are the format and fee decided?

Start by sharing your goals, location and possible dates. We’ll discuss the scope, format, availability and fee together. Sending an inquiry starts that conversation; it does not confirm a booking.

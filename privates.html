Diagnose. Understand. Transfer.

Private Brazilian Zouk and Lambada lessons are tailored to the dancer in front of me. We identify the real constraint, build a clear understanding of the movement, and turn that understanding into practice that transfers back into social dancing.

Best for

Kinesthetic Practice Session

Less talking. More dancing.

A Kinesthetic Practice Session is one-on-one Brazilian Zouk or Lambada practice designed around action rather than theory. Instead of stopping frequently for explanation, we spend more time moving, repeating, adjusting and building the skill directly through the body.

The session uses guided repetition and physical feedback to strengthen timing, connection, coordination and movement habits.

What makes it different

  • Minimal discussion
  • Continuous partnered movement
  • High repetition
  • Immediate physical feedback
  • Movement habits built through practice

Private Zouk & Lambada lessons NYC FAQ

Can I book Brazilian Zouk or Lambada?

Yes. Private training and Kinesthetic Practice Sessions can be used for Brazilian Zouk, Lambada or a combination of both.

What is the difference between a private lesson and a Kinesthetic Practice Session?

A private lesson includes more diagnosis, explanation and technical problem-solving. A Kinesthetic Practice Session minimizes discussion and prioritizes continuous movement, repetition and embodied feedback.

Who are these sessions for?

They can be adapted to developing dancers, experienced social dancers and competitors who want focused one-on-one training in New York City.

Who this is for

Choose the format that matches the problem.

Private lesson is a good fit if…

You want a technical diagnosis, detailed explanation, help understanding why something is not working, competition preparation, or a focused plan for a specific movement problem.

Kinesthetic Practice is a good fit if…

You already understand the idea but need more dancing, repetition, physical feedback, timing, connection and coordination until the skill feels more automatic.

This may not be the right format if…

You mainly want to collect new combinations without working on the underlying movement, or you are looking for a passive session with little practice or feedback.

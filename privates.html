Before your first step

A few useful answers.

Get a clearer sense of the next step, then bring your own questions to Gab.

Should I choose coaching or kinesthetic practice?

Choose a one-hour private for individual attention to a question or technical goal. A 30-minute Kinesthetic Practice Session emphasizes dancing, repetition and immediate partner feedback. If you are unsure, describe your goal below and ask Gab which format fits.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to improve?

You can begin with a question or a situation that feels difficult—such as adapting to different partners or finding your timing. Share your experience and Gab can help you choose a focus.

How do we arrange the session?

Send your goal and two or three possible times. Confirm the format, location, availability, payment and cancellation arrangements directly with Gab before attending.

Can I have support between lessons?

For ongoing guidance, explore mentorship. It connects individual sessions, group training, practice and a personal learning space.

Private lessons

Diagnose. Understand. Transfer.

Private lessons are for dancers who need individualized technical analysis, explanation and problem-solving. We identify the real constraint, build a clear model of the movement and turn that understanding into practice that can transfer back into social dancing.

  • Technique and movement mechanics
  • Connection and partner communication
  • Timing and musicality
  • Body organization, axis and pathways
  • Competition or social-dance preparation
  • Recurring technical problems and targeted drills

Best when the problem is unclear.

If you need diagnosis, conceptual clarity or a more precise correction, start with a private lesson.

Kinesthetic Practice

Less talking. More dancing.

Kinesthetic Practice is a movement-first format. Instead of stopping frequently for explanation, we spend more time moving, repeating, adjusting and building the skill directly through the body.

It is especially useful when you already understand the idea but need enough high-quality physical repetition for the movement to become more reliable.

What changes in this format

  • Minimal discussion
  • Continuous partnered movement
  • High repetition
  • Immediate physical feedback
  • Movement habits built through practice
Two complementary formats

Private lessons help you understand what to change. Kinesthetic Practice gives you more time to embody the change.

Choose the format that matches the problem

Choose Private Training if…

You want diagnosis, detailed explanation, technical problem-solving, competition preparation or a focused plan for a specific movement problem.

Choose Kinesthetic Practice if…

You understand the idea but need more repetition, partner feedback, timing, connection and coordination until it becomes more automatic.

Choose Mentorship if…

You need recurring goals, feedback, SRS, review and a longer-term training structure rather than a single session.

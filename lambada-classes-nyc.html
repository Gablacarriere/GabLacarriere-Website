Learn Lambada in NYC

Build foundations that create freedom.

Training focuses on the structures that allow dancers to move clearly, safely and creatively with a partner: rhythm, body organization, partner communication, movement mechanics, pathways and relationships between patterns. Rather than treating Lambada as a collection of figures, the goal is to build a framework dancers can reuse.

What you train

  • Lambada rhythm and timing
  • Body organization and mechanics
  • Partner communication
  • Pathways and pattern relationships
  • Movement transformations
  • Social-dance adaptability
Structured learning

Foundations

Develop rhythm, mechanics and partner communication that make later material easier.

Architecture

Understand pathways, structures and how patterns relate and transform.

FAQ

Do I need a partner?

No. You can attend independently and train through partner rotation.

Where are classes held?

Lambada Flow takes place at Ripley-Grier Studio in New York City on Thursdays. Contact Gab for the exact address and room before attending.

Can I take private lessons?

Yes. Private training can focus on rhythm, mechanics, partner communication, pathways or a specific technical challenge.

Learn Lambada in NYC

Build foundations that create freedom.

Gab’s Lambada teaching focuses on the structures that allow dancers to move clearly, safely and creatively with a partner. Students train rhythm, body organization, partner communication, movement mechanics, pathways and the relationships between patterns.

Rather than treating Lambada as a collection of figures, the training develops a framework dancers can reuse across many situations.

What you train

  • Lambada rhythm and timing
  • Body organization and mechanics
  • Partner communication
  • Pathways and pattern relationships
  • Movement transformations
  • Social-dance adaptability

Lambada NYC FAQ

Do I need a partner?

No. You can attend independently and train through partner rotation.

Where are Lambada classes held?

Gab teaches Lambada in New York City. The Classes page contains the current venue and schedule.

Can I take private Lambada lessons?

Yes. Private training can focus on rhythm, mechanics, partner communication, pathways or a specific technical challenge.

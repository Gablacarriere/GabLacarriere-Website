Before you pack

A few useful details.

Can I stay without taking private lessons?

The Zouk BNB training packages require 2 private lessons for a weekend, 3 for a week or 10 for a month. If you want accommodation only, ask about the separate Airbnb listing or room-only rates.

What level of Zouk do I need?

Tell Steph and Gab about your experience and what you want to work on. They can discuss the fit and training options before you book.

Is mentorship included in every stay?

Mentorship access is included in the monthly residency. Ask about options for your specific dates if you are planning a shorter stay.

How do I confirm a booking?

Send your dates, number of guests, preferred room and training goals. Confirm availability, the full price, lesson arrangements and payment and cancellation terms directly with your hosts. An inquiry does not reserve a room.

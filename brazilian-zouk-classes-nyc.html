Learn Brazilian Zouk in NYC

Understand the movement, not just the pattern.

Training develops the principles underneath the dance: connection, body organization, timing, pathways, transitions and the ability to adapt in real social dancing. The objective is not only to reproduce a sequence, but to understand enough of its structure that it becomes reusable.

What you train

  • Connection and partner communication
  • Timing and rhythm
  • Pattern combinations and transformations
  • Body organization and movement quality
  • Social-dance adaptability
  • JnJ-oriented decision-making and clarity
Who the classes are for

Intermediate+ level

These weekly classes are for dancers with prior experience. Contact Gab if you are unsure whether the level fits you.

Intermediate dancers

Develop combinations, transitions, musical choices and adaptable movement vocabulary.

Serious students & competitors

Train precision, decision-making, connection quality and transferable movement principles.

FAQ

Do I need a partner?

No. You can attend independently.

Where are the classes?

Tuesday classes take place at Playwright Horizons in New York City, from 7–9 PM. Contact Gab for the exact address and room before attending.

Can I book a private?

Yes. Private training is available for individualized work on technique, connection, mechanics, musicality or recurring problems.

Learn Brazilian Zouk in NYC

Understand the movement, not just the pattern.

Gab’s Brazilian Zouk classes are designed for dancers who want more than sequences to memorize. Training develops the principles underneath the dance: connection, body organization, timing, pathways, pattern architecture, transitions and the ability to adapt in real social dancing.

Classes are taught in New York City and connect technical understanding with repeatable practice so dancers can build skills that transfer from one pattern to another.

What you train

  • Connection and partner communication
  • Timing and rhythm
  • Pattern combinations and transformations
  • Body organization and movement quality
  • Social-dance adaptability
  • JnJ-oriented decision-making and clarity

Brazilian Zouk NYC FAQ

Do I need a partner?

No. Partner rotation is a normal part of social-dance training, so you can attend independently.

Where are the classes?

Gab teaches regular Brazilian Zouk classes in New York City. Check the current Classes page for the latest venue, day and time.

Can I book a private Brazilian Zouk lesson?

Yes. Private training is available for dancers who want individualized work on technique, connection, mechanics, musicality or specific recurring problems.

Kinesthetic practice

Less talking. More movement.

Kinesthetic Practice Sessions are one-on-one, movement-first sessions with minimal explanation and much more continuous dancing, repetition and physical feedback. They are designed to build timing, connection, coordination and movement habits directly through guided practice.

Teaching approach

The goal is not to collect more material. It is to identify what matters, practice it deliberately and make it available inside real dancing.

Mentorship FAQ

Common questions

Is this the same as booking private lessons?

No. Private lessons are individual sessions. The mentorship is an ongoing training structure that combines different forms of practice and keeps your development organized over time.

Is the mentorship only for Brazilian Zouk?

No. The program can include Brazilian Zouk, Lambada or both, depending on your goals.

What is a Kinesthetic Practice Session?

It is a one-on-one guided practice format with less discussion and more continuous movement, repetition and physical feedback than a traditional private lesson.

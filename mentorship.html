The learning loop

The program is organized around four recurring questions.

What matters now?

Choose a narrow current priority rather than trying to improve everything simultaneously.

What is the bottleneck?

Separate timing, perception, organization, connection and decision-making problems before prescribing practice.

When should it return?

Use retrieval and spaced review so important material comes back before it disappears.

Does it transfer?

Test the skill with different partners, contexts, entries, timing and social-dance constraints.

Teaching approach

The goal is not to collect more material. It is to identify what matters, practice it deliberately and make it available inside real dancing.

Who it is for

Developing dancers

Build reliable foundations without accumulating patterns you cannot yet organize or adapt.

Intermediate dancers

Clarify recurring technical issues, deepen movement logic and improve social-dance transfer.

Serious learners

Create a longer-term training structure around deliberate practice, feedback and measurable priorities.

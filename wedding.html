Wedding Dance Lessons · NYC

First Dance Forever.

Personalized wedding dance lessons in New York City designed to make your first dance feel natural, connected and memorable—not over-rehearsed.

Ask about wedding lessons

Built around you

Your music, experience, comfort level and personality shape the dance.

Comfort before choreography

Learn movement you can actually own so you feel present rather than worried about remembering steps.

Connection + elegance

Develop simple partnering tools, timing and visual clarity that make the dance feel polished without becoming artificial.

The process

A first dance that feels like you.

  • Choose or refine your song
  • Assess your current comfort and experience
  • Build a structure appropriate to the music
  • Practice transitions, timing and connection
  • Rehearse until the dance feels reliable rather than fragile
For couples

No dance background required.

The goal is not to turn your wedding into a performance exam. Training is adapted to your available time and to how simple or ambitious you want the first dance to be.

FAQ

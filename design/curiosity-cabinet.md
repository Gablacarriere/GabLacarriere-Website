# Creative direction: the curiosity cabinet
Saved September 11, 2026.

## References and what we borrowed
- Bruno Simon — https://bruno-simon.com/ — a portfolio explored as a place, with objects and discoveries. Adopt the idea of destinations with personality, using clear links and a lightweight scene selector.
- Patatap — https://patatap.com/ — touch connects abstract shapes and sound. Adopt a small optional three-note musical toy: no autoplay, no rhythm grading, no imitation of its artwork or recordings.
- Rijksmuseum Stories — https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/stories — curiosity-led journeys into stories, research and individual works. Give the Journal a visible editorial doorway, with an actual article to read.
- Active Theory — https://v5.activetheory.net/ — atmospheric digital environments. Use atmosphere through original shapes, a restrained palette and brief motion.

## Implemented
Homepage section immediately after its introduction. Four rooms: dance floor, playground, reading room, observatory. Each has its own original vector composition and useful primary/secondary destination.
Warm paper, sage, terracotta, lavender, muted gold and slate. Serif display typography, plain small labels. Art may echo cut paper and kinetic sculpture without reproducing a particular work.
The public cabinet leads into the existing Zoukable and Zouk Atlas worlds. The site remains about dance education, and visitors can still directly find training options.

## Interaction rules
- Mouse, touch and keyboard activation through native buttons and links.
- Selected scene marked with aria-pressed, panel updates announced politely.
- Sound off initially; separate opt-in. Short synthesized tones only after a note is pressed.
- No autoplay carousel. Brief motion on scene selection. Respect reduced motion.
- No new personal data, tracking or scores.
- Preserve fallback navigation when scripts are unavailable.
- Original artwork and synthesized audio; no borrowed copyrighted assets.

Biography

For Gab, technique is not the end goal. It is the structure that makes freedom possible.

Originally from France, Gab came to dance with a background in pedagogy and philosophy of science, together with professional experience as a school teacher. That foundation continues to shape his approach to movement education through observation, diagnosis, sequencing, feedback and learning design.

He began teaching Brazilian Zouk in France around 2017–2018, first in Rennes and later in Paris. His early professional years included a long-running collaboration with Sabrina Beija-Flor, combining regular Brazilian Zouk classes, workshops, social events and European appearances, alongside his developing work as DJ Gab.

He later developed a teaching and performance partnership with Patrycja Jakimczyk, teaching Brazilian Zouk and Lambada in Paris and appearing at workshops, festivals and dance events across Europe.

In 2023, Gab began collaborating with Danish dancer and educator Caroline Haugsted. They became official dance partners in 2024 and developed Zouk Art Empowerment, combining technical clarity with creative exploration, expression and embodied connection.

Now based in New York City, Gab teaches Brazilian Zouk and Lambada through weekly classes, workshops, private coaching, Kinesthetic Practice Sessions and long-term mentorship.
